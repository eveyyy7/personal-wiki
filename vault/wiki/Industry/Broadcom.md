# Broadcom

> Broadcom's role in turning hyperscaler requirements into manufacturable silicon.

## Summary

Hyperscalers define what a custom chip must do; Broadcom helps turn those requirements into scalable, manufacturable silicon, contributing silicon implementation, high-speed I/O, SerDes and networking IP. It may benefit twice: from building the accelerators and from the networking and connectivity around them.

## Key points

- Hyperscalers own the AI model, workload, performance targets, and software stack, while Broadcom handles silicon implementation and manufacturability *(source: [[podcast-asics-broadcom-nvlink]] · PART 4 — WHERE BROADCOM COMES IN)*
- Broadcom contributes in areas like high-speed I/O, SerDes, networking IP, and packaging integration *(source: [[podcast-asics-broadcom-nvlink]] · PART 4 — WHERE BROADCOM COMES IN)*
- Broadcom can benefit from custom ASIC growth by helping hyperscalers build the accelerator or by supplying surrounding networking and connectivity *(source: [[podcast-asics-broadcom-nvlink]] · PART 11 — WHY THIS MATTERS FOR BROADCOM)*
- Broadcom's revenue is not limited to ASICs; it can earn money from infrastructure like SerDes, Ethernet, switching, and optical connectivity *(source: [[podcast-asics-broadcom-nvlink]] · PART 11 — WHY THIS MATTERS FOR BROADCOM)*
