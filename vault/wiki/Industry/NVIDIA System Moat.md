# NVIDIA System Moat

> NVIDIA's moat extends beyond the GPU to its entire integrated system.

## Summary

NVIDIA's competitive advantage stems from its complete system, which integrates components like the GPU, CUDA, NVLink, and NVSwitch. This comprehensive nature makes the platform difficult for customers to replace, even if a competitor offers a comparable GPU. The consideration of software, libraries, interconnect, and system architecture solidifies this moat.
